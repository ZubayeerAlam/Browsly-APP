## What we collect

| Data | Why we need it |
|---|---|
| Name and profile photo | To show who you are |
| Email address, phone number, or Google sign-in details | To sign you in and verify your account |
| Delivery addresses, with recipient name and phone number | To deliver your orders |
| Orders, reviews and reports | To run the marketplace and keep records |
| Chat messages | To let buyers and sellers talk |
| Shop details and any bKash or contact number you add | To run your shop and settle commission |
| Products you view or click, with a random session ID | To show sellers their statistics |
| Device token | To send push notifications |

We do not collect card or bank details in the app. Buyers pay cash on delivery, and sellers pay commission outside the app.

## Who can see your data

- **Everyone, without signing in:** your shop's public details, your listings and their photos, and your reviews.
- **The seller of your order:** your recipient name, phone number and delivery address, so they can deliver it. They see only their own part of your order.
- **The other person in a chat:** your name and your messages.
- **Browsly admins:** account and order details needed to run the service. Admins read a chat message only if someone has reported it.

Photos you upload are public to anyone with the link. We remove location and camera details from them before upload.

## How long we keep it

- Your profile, addresses, wishlist, cart, notifications and photos are kept until you delete your account.
- Records of which products were viewed or clicked are kept for 90 days, then only daily totals remain.
- Device tokens that have not been used for a long time are removed automatically.
- Photos that nothing uses any more are removed automatically after a few days.
- Order records are kept after you delete your account, with your name and address removed, because the seller and Browsly need them for accounts. [CONFIRM THE RETENTION PERIOD WITH YOUR ADVISER.]

## Deleting your account

Go to Settings, then Account & Security, then Delete account. You cannot delete your account while you have open orders or owe commission. Your conversations and shop are deleted with it. You can also ask us at [CONTACT EMAIL].

## Your choices

You can change your name, photo and addresses in the app at any time. You can ask us for a copy of your data, or to correct it, at [CONTACT EMAIL].
